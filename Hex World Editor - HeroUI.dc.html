HeroUI 3 separates by surface layering, not borders: background oklch(12%), surface, surface-secondary, then --segment for the selected pill. So the rail, drawer and inspector become floating panels with a 10px gutter instead of hairline-divided columns.
Radii come from --radius: 0.5rem: 24px cards and menus, 12px controls and fields, 999px chips.
Selected states use --accent-soft tint + soft-foreground text; the solid accent is reserved for the one primary action per view.
